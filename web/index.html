PRIVACY FIRST

Your information belongs to you.

In this version, saved reports and a journey check-in record remain in your browser. We do not collect registration data or run a location-tracking backend.

◉
Location access

Only when you explicitly tap the location button or opt in to sharing.

◇
Provider requests

Live route requests send endpoints to Valhalla (or OpenRouteService when configured). Explicit place and lighting lookups send limited coordinates to OSM providers. Map tiles reveal the viewport. Third parties have independent privacy policies.

◎
No public female activity map

This prototype has no user-location feed, no gender counts, and no background tracking.

✧
Personal data controls

You can export or erase the reports and journey history held on this device.

⚠ This is an early research release, not an emergency dispatch service. No route is certified safe.
