BindaasRaah v0.2 — Quick Start
==============================

1. Extract bindaasraah-v02.zip in Downloads.
2. Terminal:
     cd ~/Downloads/bindaasraah-v02
     python3 server.py
3. Browser: http://localhost:8765

If your old v0.1 server is already running, press Control+C in its Terminal,
OR run:
     PORT=8766 python3 server.py
and open http://localhost:8766

What is real: OpenStreetMap map tiles, geocoding on search, public Valhalla
walking/driving routes (best effort), explicit nearby/along-route OSM place
lookups and sampled OSM streetlight lit tags. Requires internet.

What is NOT real or deployed: verified safety rating, live gender/phone counts,
working/staffed help verification, women-only user verification, matching,
private chat or emergency dispatch. Demo fallback cards are fictional.

For better route reliability use your own OpenRouteService key:
     export ORS_API_KEY='your_key'
     python3 server.py

For immediate danger in India call 112. Read README.md first.
